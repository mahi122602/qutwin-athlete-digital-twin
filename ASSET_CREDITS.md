# Asset sources

The original photographic style references and distant panorama were generated
earlier in this QUTwin conversation. They are reference imagery, not evidence of
a corresponding finished 3D model.

## Poly Haven — CC0

These geometric assets are distributed in processed glTF form with textures:

- https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01
- https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02
- https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small
- https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04
- https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02
- https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01
- https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_09

Surface and lighting sources:

- https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
- https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_tiles
- https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
- https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunrise

Poly Haven asset licence: https://polyhaven.com/license

Individual 3D leaves are retained as contour geometry. The original photographic
textures are encoded for browser delivery; image encoding is not claimed as
new original photography. Some assets and textures include unused variants to
support further editing.

## MakeHuman system assets — CC0

Clothed character geometry and skin, eye, brow, hair and clothing textures were
prepared from the official MakeHuman system assets pack. This is a constructed
human model, not a photographic likeness of a real person.

- https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md

The MPFB add-on was a build-time tool. The package does not distribute the add-on
or Blender as application runtime dependencies.

## Three.js — MIT

Three.js 0.160.1 and its Water helper are bundled in the prepared frontend.
The water normal texture is from the Three.js examples. Its MIT licence is
included at `build_source/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
