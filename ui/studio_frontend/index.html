How these groups are assigned

Current fatigue: high ≥70, medium 40–69.99, low <40. Injury uses the saved experimental category. Forecast uses its existing rules: high if fatigue ≥80, readiness <40 or injury score ≥75; medium if fatigue ≥65, readiness <60 or injury score ≥45; otherwise low. These are application research bands, not clinically validated thresholds.

Coach review priorities are separate from health risk: declined drafts are reviewed first, revised or unapproved drafts need review, and approved advice is routine follow-up. A forecast must be saved after the latest upload and contain dated upcoming values. Nothing missing is treated as low risk.
